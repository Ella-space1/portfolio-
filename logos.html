Logos

I designed these three logos as an intern at K&S Design Company. All three were selected and used on business cards.

The first is for K&S itself. It uses Gam (☵), the trigram for water on the Korean flag, for the company's plan to expand abroad like flowing water.

The second, for 한국이엔테크, reflects the company's initials and core values.

The third, for Haedam Industrial Development, combines the initials H and D. The balance between them refers to the company's focus on looks and structure in architecture.
