About

I'm Ella Lee, a graphic designer studying Communication Design at Parsons School of Design, class of 2027. I work in print and branding.

For UDCenter, I designed the plaque that Seongnam gives to restaurants and cafes that install wheelchair ramps. The design was later adapted for a similar project in Hwaseong. As an intern at K&S Design Company, I designed three logos. All three were selected and used on business cards.

At school, I designed a book from Wikipedia's "Magic" entry, laid out like a modern grimoire, and a poster based on Apple's "Here's to the crazy ones" text.
