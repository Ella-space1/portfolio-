Plaque

I designed this plaque for UDCenter, a company that specializes in facilities for people with disabilities. It was part of a nationwide accessibility project.

The city of Seongnam gives it to businesses, such as restaurants and cafes, that install wheelchair ramps. The design was later adapted for a similar project in Hwaseong.
